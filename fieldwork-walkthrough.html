  1. 0:00

    The problem

    A delivery brief can hide more work than a team can handle, or unclear review rules. Fieldwork makes these assumptions visible. This illustrated walkthrough uses screenshots from Dhairya’s independent portfolio demo.

  2. 0:14

    An editable brief

    The planning lab starts with a fictional brief: item volume, time per item, review coverage and team availability. Each input is an editable assumption, making the estimate straightforward to inspect and question.

  3. 0:29

    Capacity without hidden promises

    In this preset, estimated work is sixty-six hours against seventy-five available. Production and review share that capacity. Onboarding, pilot work, rework and coordination are excluded, so the difference is not guaranteed spare time.

  4. 0:46

    Checks that match the plan

    Unconfirmed permissions or unclear acceptance rules remain blockers. The checklist records example review acknowledgements, not verified consent. Editing the brief clears those acknowledgements, preventing an outdated review from carrying over to a changed plan.

  5. 1:01

    A clear review owner

    The separate review dashboard shows fictional submissions and their owners. A submission needs an assigned reviewer before review can start. Clear status rules describe what can happen next and what information is missing.

  6. 1:15

    Explain each decision

    Acceptance and requests for changes both need a written note. The workflow supports another submission and review after changes. Accepted records become read-only, while session activity provides a visible account of those decisions.

  7. 1:32

    What the demonstration proves

    Together, these demos show a planning handoff and an organized review queue. They use fixed rules and fictional data, with AI-assisted development. There are no live business connections, and browser changes disappear on refresh.

  8. 1:47

    A focused paid starting point

    Start with a paid review of one workflow: its steps, exceptions and acceptance checks. Dhairya can scope that review first, with any implementation priced separately after requirements and tests are agreed.
